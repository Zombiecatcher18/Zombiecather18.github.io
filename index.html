Project R — Turn-Based RPG with QTE Combat

An original RPG in active development. Players build a combo from a hand of moves, then execute each move through a timed QTE challenge. Combat rewards timing, sequencing, and reading enemy patterns.

Systems I built:

  • Turn-based battle loop with alternating turns and a tug-of-war turn meter
  • QTE-driven combo execution (Timing bar + Button prompt variants)
  • Enemy AI with awareness → prediction → counter systems
  • Move data model with synergy and anti-synergy chains
  • Bounty / level-up progression integrated with combat
  • Full battle lifecycle from setup through victory or defeat
